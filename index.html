Do I need Obsidian?+

Yes. AI ChatBridge and OneNote Style are plugins for the desktop version of Obsidian. Brain Capture is an Android app being developed to save to an Obsidian vault. Obsidian is a notes app; a vault is simply the folder where your notes live. Get Obsidian here ↗.

Can I try these in my browser?+

This page introduces the tools. ChatBridge and OneNote Style run inside Obsidian on your computer, and their setup guides are behind each “Explore” button. Brain Capture is still a proof of concept, with no public download yet.

Can I use the tools separately?+

Absolutely. Use ChatBridge to keep AI conversations, OneNote Style for familiar navigation, or both. OneNote Style also includes ChatBridge sync buttons when both plugins are installed.
